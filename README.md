# Aldo Malasomma

**AI Systems & Creative Technology Developer** · Tallinn, Estonia

I design AI systems and experimental software with an emphasis on modular architectures, observable behavior, and human oversight. My work connects multi-agent coordination, memory research, computational experimentation, and creative technology.

## Areas of Focus

- **Multi-agent orchestration** — provider adapters, workflow coordination, handoffs, and human review.
- **Memory architectures and cognitive systems** — retrieval, context assembly, and experimental cognitive pipelines.
- **AI research and experimental computing** — theoretical frameworks, deterministic simulations, and traceable policy experiments.
- **Software architecture and structured creative systems** — typed domain models, API boundaries, validation, and graph-based organization.
- **Creative technology** — interactive audiovisual systems and tools for structured creative work.

## Research

The [Reflexive Coherence Model (RCM)](https://github.com/AldoMalasomma/reflexive-coherence-model) is an independent theoretical research program investigating candidate organizational conditions relevant to consciousness and reflexive information dynamics. It also explores their temporal dynamics and computational operationalization; its proposed constructs and relationships require empirical investigation and are not validated measures or demonstrations of consciousness in artificial systems.
